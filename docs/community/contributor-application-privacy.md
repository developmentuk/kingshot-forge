# Contributor application privacy notice

This notice is a project policy draft for the future secure application workflow, not legal advice. Owner/legal review is required before collecting applications.

The planned workflow should collect only information needed to assess a contribution path, communicate about the application, record agreements and support onboarding. It should not collect exact date of birth. The initial project policy is that applicants must confirm they are at least 18; under-18 applications are not accepted until a safeguarding policy exists.

Access should be limited to authorised recruitment reviewers. Applicant-visible messages must remain separate from internal review notes. Retention, withdrawal, deletion/anonymisation and security controls must be implemented and verified through Supabase RLS, grants and audit events before the form is enabled.

The project contact route must be configurable. No fabricated company address or unsupported legal claim is published here. The current public catalogue collects no application information.
