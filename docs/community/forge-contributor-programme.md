# Forge Contributors programme

Kingshot Forge is an unofficial, community-run hobby project. Forge Contributors are unpaid volunteers who choose to help in their free time. These roles are not employment, do not provide wages or benefits, and do not promise future payment, fixed hours or acceptance.

## How contribution works

Contributions are flexible and remote/asynchronous. There are no fixed hours. Most contributors take on small tasks when they have time. Reliability and communication are more important than a particular weekly commitment.

Work is scoped through reviewed issues, branches and pull requests where appropriate. Access is progressive and capability-based. Production credentials, secrets, deployment administration and private reports are never granted automatically.

## Recognition

Where appropriate, contributors may receive community recognition, a contributor profile or badge, portfolio-friendly acknowledgements, participation in planning and testing, and acknowledgement in Forge credits. Recognition is not compensation and is not guaranteed.

## Current foundation

The public role catalogue is available at `/join`. The secure application, review, onboarding and contributor-profile persistence workflow is not yet implemented on this branch. No application personal information is collected by the public catalogue.

## Principles

Contributors follow the [Contributor Code of Conduct](contributor-code-of-conduct.md), [application privacy notice](contributor-application-privacy.md) and [contribution terms](contribution-terms.md). Contributors can step away, and access may be removed when security, safety or conduct requires it.
