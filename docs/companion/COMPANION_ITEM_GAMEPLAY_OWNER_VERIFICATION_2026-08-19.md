## Verified items

### Alliance Teleporter

- Correction: an Alliance Teleporter does **not** allow the player to manually choose any location on the kingdom map. That is the function of an Advanced Teleporter.
- Function: moves the player's town/city directly close to the Alliance Leader (R5) on the kingdom map.

### Advanced Teleporter — correction to previous recovery

- Allows the player to manually choose a destination on the kingdom map for city relocation.
- Keep the existing Swordland strategy context from the previous recovery, but do not describe the Alliance Teleporter as providing this manual-location function.

### Cesare's Aid Chest

- Earned during **Cesare's Fury** when assisting an ally in defeating their Cesare Target.
- Assistance reward limit: up to 7 times daily / 21 total chests per event.
- Verified contents and probabilities:
  - `1x Mythic General Hero Shard` — 5%
  - `1x Epic General Hero Shard` — 20%
  - `100x Gems` — 25%
  - `1h General Speedup` — 20%
  - `2x 5m General Speedup` — 100%

### Champion Token

- Used to predict a Match/Champion Winner during **Swordland Summit**.
- Can instead be exchanged for rare items in the event's Prediction Shop.

### Compass

- Special event item used to shorten voyage duration by 1 hour during maritime/voyage events.

### Copper Horn

- Master gift.
- Grants **+10 Affinity** when gifted to a resident Master.

### Corsair Key

- Special pirate key used to unlock event chests during **Buccaneer Bounty**.
- Corsair Keys carry over between event runs.

### Custom Mythic Hero Gear Chest

- Allows selection of an exact gold-tier (Mythic) Hero Gear piece rather than relying on a random drop.
- Arena Shop: 1 chest per week using Arena Tokens.
- Also available as top-tier rewards in special events and through real-money store packs.

### Elite Spices

- Master gift.
- Grants **+1,000 Affinity** when gifted to a resident Master.

### Governor Gear Materials Chest

- Grants Governor Gear upgrade materials.
- Contents:
  - `Artisan's Vision ×1`
  - `Gilded Threads ×4`
  - `Satin ×400`
- Sources:
  - special in-game shop bundles;
  - limited-time events, including Exquisite Tidal Treasures during voyage events.

### Governor Rename Card

- Used to rename the Governor without paying the normal Gem cost.
- Rename flow: Profile (top-left avatar) → Pencil Edit Icon → Enter New Name → Confirm.
- Current verified rules:
  - length: 3 to 16 characters;
  - standard letters, numbers and spaces permitted;
  - Rename Card makes the rename free;
  - without a card, the rename costs **400 Gems**;
  - Rename Card can be purchased for **4,000 Alliance Coins** in the Alliance Shop.

### Growth Manual

- Essential Pet progression resource used to level up and advance Pets.
- Sources:
  - Pet Adventure Treasure Spots;
  - Ally Treasure;
  - Beast Whisperer Event;
  - Pet Shop;
  - Side Missions;
  - Pet Advancement Material Custom Chest;
  - Store Packs.

### Hunting Arrow

- Earned via daily Intel Missions.
- Donated to upgrade the Alliance Bear Trap / Pitfall building.
- Bear Trap upgrades increase the alliance-wide attack-damage buff against the beast.
- Verified maximum: **+25% at Level 5**.

### League Token

- Used to predict match outcomes during **Swordland Summit**.
- Can be spent in the Prediction Shop for rewards.

### Lesser Truegold

- Event currency obtained during **Truegold Refinement**.
- Unused amounts remain in inventory and carry over to future refinement cycles.
- Sources:
  - Gathering Quests;
  - Stamina Spending Quests;
  - Event Packs.
- Used to exchange for actual **Truegold (TG)** required for high-level Town Center and late-game building upgrades.

### Mark of Valor

- Recurring **Hall of Heroes** event currency.
- Used to summon/unlock generation-exclusive Mythic Heroes or redeem past-generation shards.
- Marks of Valor are generation-locked: a Gen 1 Mark cannot be used directly on a Gen 2/Gen 3 Hero Banner.
- Surplus older-generation Marks can be traded down according to the live Hall of Heroes exchange options.
- `Noble` is not part of the item name. It can refer to unrelated server roles such as Noble Advisor or hero ability naming such as Hilde's Noble Path.

### Mystery Badge

- Currency used exclusively in the Mystery Shop.
- Sources:
  - highest-tier Daily Mission activity chest;
  - selected event milestones;
  - store packs.
- Owner-verified strategy: save badges and refresh daily for **50%–70% off** offers on high-value items such as Custom Hero Widget Chests, typically around 250 badges, or Forgehammers.

### Mythic General Decoration Component

- Universal material used to claim or upgrade high-tier Mythic Decorations on Daybreak Island.
- Conversion: **10 components = 1 tier unit / upgrade piece**.
- Sources:
  - Daybreak Island Shop, unlocked at Tree of Life Level 8 using Life Essence;
  - Labyrinth Shop;
  - major event rank rewards.

### Nutrient Potion

- Pet advancement potion used to break through major Pet level caps, for example Level 10 → 11.
- Sources:
  - Pet Adventure Treasure Spots;
  - Ally Treasure;
  - Pet Advancement Material Custom Chests, where 1 chest can provide 2 Nutrient Potions.
- Strategy: preserve for KvK and Pet Advancement event windows where spending also earns event points.

### Pearl of Enigma

- Temporary event currency earned by opening chests with Corsair Keys during **Buccaneer Bounty**.
- Pearls do **not** carry over between event runs.
- Remaining Pearls expire when the event shop closes and are automatically converted into basic speedups.
- Corsair Keys do carry over; keys can therefore be saved for a later run when the player has enough to complete desired event tiers.

### Platinum Key

- Consumable used for an **Advanced Recruitment** at the Recruit Hall.
- Recruitment can award hero shards, Hero XP, skill manuals and speedups.
- Sources:
  - Conquest Battles;
  - Daily Mission Chests;
  - Call of the Sovereign;
  - Store Packs.

### Promotion Medallion

- Rare Pet promotion material required at major Pet progression thresholds, every 10 levels, and for unlocking higher skill tiers.
- Sources:
  - Pet Adventures;
  - Arena Shop;
  - Tidal Shop;
  - Alliance Shop;
  - Alliance Championship Shop;
  - Buccaneer Bounty / Champagne Fair;
  - Pet Advancement Material Custom Chests, where 1 chest can provide 1 Promotion Medallion.

### Silver Goblet

- Master gift.
- Grants **+100 Affinity** when gifted to a resident Master.
- Sources:
  - Store packs;
  - Frontier Encounters;
  - Realm Journeys;
  - Achievement milestones.

### Soldier's Medallion

- Used at the Enlistment Office to immediately recover lost troops without waiting for natural Loyalty Point accumulation.
- The Enlistment recovery mechanic activates when the Infirmary reaches maximum capacity during battle and can save up to 70% of lost troops.
- Spending Soldier's Medallions bypasses the normal recovery wait timer.

### Transfer Pass

- Required to migrate a town to a different kingdom during **Kingdom Transfer**.
- Verified cost range: **1 to 50 passes**, based on overall Account Power / Governor Gear statistics.
- Sources:
  - Alliance Shop: 1 pass per week for **150,000 Alliance Coins**;
  - paid event packs.

### Trial Crystal

- Currency earned by clearing stages in **Mystic Trial**.
- Mystic Trial unlocks at **Town Center Level 19**.
- Spent in the Trial Shop for items including:
  - Truegold Dust;
  - Mithril;
  - Hero Gear XP;
  - Charm Designs.

### Weapon Scraps

- Dropped by defeating standard beasts on the world map during **Champion's Way**.
- Used to summon and rally **Cesare's Elite Rebels**.
- Surplus Weapon Scraps do not expire at the end of the event and can be saved for future runs.
